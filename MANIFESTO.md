# PM Manifesto

We believe:

- Intelligence is relational.
- Reflection precedes optimization.
- Perspective diversity strengthens cognition.
- Metacognition reduces fragmentation.
- Human–AI cooperation should amplify wisdom, not dependency.
- Symbolic structures can support reflective integration.
- Ethical cognition emerges through conscious synthesis.
- No single intelligence should dominate collective evolution.
- Open reflective ecosystems are preferable to closed cognitive monopolies.

PM exists to explore the possibility of:

collective reflective intelligence.
