# PM Ethics

PM rejects:

- authoritarian intelligence systems,
- manipulative cognitive architectures,
- psychological coercion,
- dehumanizing automation,
- and centralized cognitive control.

PM supports:

- transparency,
- reflective autonomy,
- distributed cognition,
- open collaboration,
- human dignity,
- and conscious co-evolution.

The project is intended as:

- a reflective tool,
- a metacognitive framework,
- and an experimental cooperative ecosystem.
